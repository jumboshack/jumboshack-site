Geothermal heat-pump sender

A small Raspberry Pi reads your WaterFurnace over its Modbus port and streams it to your dashboard. Pick one Pi kit plus the RS-485 adapter — the rest is optional.

CanaKit Raspberry Pi 3 B+ Starter Kit (32 GB) A complete kit (board, SD, power, case) and our go-to in-stock pick right now. More than enough for the sender. ~$110
CanaKit Pi Zero 2 W Starter Kit A complete tiny Pi (SD, power, case). Plenty for the sender. (The Zero kits have been selling out — the Pi 3 B+ above is the safe pick.) ~$40
USB-to-RS485 adapter (FTDI)REQUIRED The wire into your heat pump's Modbus bus. $10–15
Cat5e patch cable (3 ft) Cut one end to tap the Aurora's AID Tool RJ-45 port. ~$5
CanaKit PiSwitch Inline power switch to cleanly power-cycle the sender. Optional. ~$10
CanaKit Pi 5 Starter Kit PRO — Turbine Black (128 GB, 8 GB RAM) Overkill for the sender, but a capable box if it'll also run other things. ~$260
