How it works

Three steps from hardware to live graphs.

Get a device token

Create an account, add a device, and copy your one-time jsk_… token.

Send your readings

POST JSON to the ingest API — from your own Pi, our open pipeline, or a ready-to-run Jumboshack kit. ISO-8601 timestamps, a ?validate=1 dry run, and clear docs.

Watch it live

Your dashboard, history, cost, and diagram fill in automatically — from the first reading on.
